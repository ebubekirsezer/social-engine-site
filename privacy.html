Privacy policy — ESS Cartoons Publisher

ESS Cartoons Publisher is a private automation that uploads videos to the ESS Cartoons YouTube channel on behalf of its owner.

  • It is used only by the channel owner. No other users sign in.
  • It accesses the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos, set their metadata and manage the channel's playlists.
  • It does not collect, store or share any personal data of viewers or third parties.
  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
